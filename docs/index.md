# MOMMYSHIP
**A Homebrew Table Ruleset** for **Mothership Sci-Fi Horror RPG**

---

Welcome to Mommyship! This is a mildly friendlier, broader, more robust, and ever-so-slightly less deadly take on the [Mothership® RPG](https://www.tuesdayknightgames.com/pages/mothership-rpg). This site contains the complete homebrew ruleset, split into 3 parts:

- **[Player's Survival Guide](players-survival-guide/index.md):** Character creation, Stats, Skills, classes, species, combat, & gear.
- **[Shipbreaker's Toolkit](shipbreakers-toolkit/index.md):** Ships, Crewmembers, space travel, operating costs, & ship combat.
- **[D.A.V.E. Pilot Manual](dave-pilot-manual.md):** Design & pilot your very own **D**efense **A**nd **V**iolent **E**ncounter droid!

Mommyship was created by two big nerds, **Jaz** & **Sam**, to fill some gaps in the original system. It's since branched out a fair bit. Much love & respect to the OG, of course.
